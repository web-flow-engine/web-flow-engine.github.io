Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

Introduction

LearnColoring ("we", "our", "us") is a coloring and learning app for children ages 1 to 5. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the app, how it is used, and the choices available to you. By using LearnColoring you agree to the practices described here.

The short version

We do not collect names, email addresses, contact lists, photos, location, camera, microphone input or any other personal information from your child or your device. The only information that leaves your device is an anonymous crash report, generated only when the app crashes. We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt, and we do not sell or share data with anyone for advertising.

What stays on your device

Coloring pages, drawing progress, your settings (drawing mode, sound, narration, screen-time preferences) and your purchase entitlements are stored locally on the device. Purchased content packs are downloaded once and cached on the device so they work without a connection. None of this is transmitted to us.

Data we collect

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, child-safe mode): if the app crashes, we receive the stack trace, the app version, the device model and OS version, and a non-personal context map needed to diagnose the fault. Crashlytics is configured in child-safe mode: it does not collect identity information, and it does not enable analytics, advertising or session tracking.
  • Usage data from ads (Google AdMob): ad requests carry device-level information such as the device model, OS version, language, screen size and IP address, together with whether an ad was loaded, shown or tapped. AdMob may derive coarse location from the IP address. See "Advertising" below.
  • Purchases: the store (Apple App Store or Google Play) shares a non-personally-identifying product identifier and a purchase confirmation so the app can unlock the item and restore it across your devices. We never see your payment details, and no receipt data leaves the device beyond that identifier.

No data is collected for analytics purposes. There is no analytics SDK in the app, and we do not operate a backend of our own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

LearnColoring is free and shows a small number of neutral ads. All ad requests are flagged for child-directed treatment, which neutralizes ad personalization, remarketing and interest-based categories. Ads are shown only on the page picker and the packs screen - never inside the coloring canvas or during the celebration.

Ads are not suitable for very young children and are shown behind the parental gate when they unlock paid content. You can remove ads entirely with the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase, available from the packs screen or Settings, and no ads are shown afterwards.

For details on how Google uses information from apps that use its services, see the Google Partner Sites policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy (COPPA, Google Play Families, Apple Kids Category)

LearnColoring is designed for children under 13 and is built to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Google Play Families Policy and the Apple App Store Kids Category requirements. Specifically:

  • No third-party tracking SDKs are included - no advertising SDK identifiers, no attribution SDKs, no social SDKs.
  • Ad requests are child-directed, so no personalized or interest-based advertising is served.
  • No persistent identifiers are used for advertising.
  • The App Store privacy labels declare "Data Not Collected", "Data Not Linked to You" and "Data Not Used to Track You" for every category.
  • Every purchase, restore and settings entry is protected by a parental gate (a simple addition problem) so a child cannot start a transaction or change settings.
  • There is no chat, no user account, and no link that leads out of the app.

Do Not Track / tracking choice

LearnColoring does not access the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not display the App Tracking Transparency prompt. We do not sell user data and we do not perform cross-app tracking, so in App Store Connect we declare that LearnColoring does not track users.

Data retention

Crash reports are retained by Google for a limited period so that defects can be fixed, after which they are deleted or anonymized. Advertising data is retained by AdMob only as long as required for billing, fraud prevention and frequency capping. We retain no data on infrastructure of our own, because we operate no backend.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction - for example the EEA, the United Kingdom or California - you may have rights to access, delete or correct data, and to object to its processing. Because LearnColoring does not hold personal data on our servers, please direct data-subject requests to the relevant processors:

  • Google (Crashlytics, AdMob): myaccount.google.com
  • Apple (in-app purchases): privacy.apple.com

For anything else, contact us at the address below and we will help route the request.

International transfers

The limited data described above is processed by Google and Apple on their global infrastructure, which may include countries outside your own. Those providers operate under their own transfer frameworks, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses. LearnColoring itself does not transfer your data internationally, because we never receive it.

Security

We rely on the security practices of Google and Apple to protect the small amount of data that flows through their systems. Your child's drawings and settings never leave the device, so they are not exposed to network risks on our side.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or the applicable law changes. Material changes will be reflected in an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.
