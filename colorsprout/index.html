How it works

Three taps to a finished picture

The whole loop is built for small hands and short attention spans. No reading required.

Pick a picture

Browse colorful themes of animals, vehicles, food and more. Every page shows a bold line-art preview with a golden star when it is finished.

Tap to fill

Choose a crayon from the tray, then tap a region. It fills with a squishy pop and speaks the name of what was colored.

Watch it come alive

Finish the page and the scene wakes up: the elephant waves, the fish swims, the sun shines, with confetti and a cheerful sound.

Why parents like it

Built like a toy, not like homework

Molded-plastic buttons, chunky crayons and forgiving touch targets - the kind of interface a toddler can bully and still win.

Tap-to-fill

Designed for ages 1-3: tap a region and it fills with the chosen color, with a squishy pop and the region's name spoken aloud.

Free draw

Switch to free draw for scribbling with a finger or stylus. A thicker brush and a smaller palette keep little hands on target.

Voice narration

Object names and color names are spoken gently as your child plays. Turn the voice off in one tap from Settings.

Animated reward

Finishing a page triggers a short celebration with confetti, a star stamp and the subject's own sound.

Big safe targets

Every control is at least 64x64px with a 72x72px touch frame, and edge margins stop palm touches from firing a tool.

Kid-safe by default

No chat, no links out of the app, no third-party tracking SDKs and a math gate in front of anything that costs money.

The learning hook

Every page teaches something

Not a curriculum. Just delight that quietly builds vocabulary, one spoken word at a time.

1Object naming

Every region has a name. Tapping a colored region speaks it aloud, reinforcing vocabulary in context.

"Elephant!"

2Color naming

Choosing and applying a color speaks its name, so color words become part of the play, not a lesson.

"Beautiful red!"

3Animated reward

Finishing a page brings the subject to life, creating a positive emotional link between your child and the thing they just colored.

"Beautiful!"
Trust

Safe by design

LearnColoring is built to the App Store Kids Category and the Google Play Families Policy, and audited against COPPA.

  • No third-party tracking. No analytics SDK beyond anonymous crash reports, and no advertising IDs are accessed.
  • Child-directed ads only. Ad requests are flagged for child-directed treatment, so no personalized or interest-based ads are served.
  • Parental gate. Every purchase, restore and settings entry is behind a simple math challenge an adult can answer and a toddler cannot.
  • No chat, no outbound links. Nothing in the app can send your child somewhere else, and no personal data is collected.
  • One-time purchase. Remove the ads forever with a single non-consumable purchase. No subscriptions, ever.
  • Screen-time limits. A gentle reminder from Settings keeps sessions short and healthy.
