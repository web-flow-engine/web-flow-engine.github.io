How the app is built

Frequently asked questions

The default experience is designed for children ages 1 to 3: tap-to-fill coloring, no reading required, very large targets and short sessions. Parents of slightly older children can switch to free draw in Settings, which suits ages 3 to 5.

Three light-touch hooks: tapping a colored region speaks its name ("Elephant!"), picking a colour speaks the colour name ("Beautiful red!"), and finishing a page brings the scene to life with a celebration. It is delight that happens to teach, not a curriculum.

Everything that costs money or opens settings is behind a simple addition problem - the parental gate. An adult can answer it easily; a toddler cannot, so purchases cannot be started by accident.

Buy the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase from the packs screen or Settings. It is a non-consumable, so it stays with you on every device signed into the same store account, and there is no subscription to cancel.

Ten themes are free and bundled with the app: Animals, Birds, Bugs, Farm, Flowers, Food, Fruits, Trees, Underwater and Vehicles. Three paid packs - Dinosaurs, Fairytale and Space - are available as one-time purchases and are downloaded once and cached on the device.

Yes. Settings has separate toggles for sound effects and music, and for voice narration, so you can keep the music and drop the talking - or the other way round.

No names, contacts, photos, location or identifiers. The only data that leaves the device is an anonymous crash report when the app crashes. Ads are served in child-directed mode, so no personalized or interest-based advertising is used, and no advertising ID is accessed. Read the full Privacy Policy.

iOS and Android from day one, built with Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform. The layout is designed for landscape on phones and small tablets, with fixed tool trays and a scaling canvas so the artwork always stays in the safe area.
