Shapes & elevation

Pill-shaped corners, chunky bevels and a physical extrusion model instead of blur elevation. Sharp edges are forbidden.

Chunky button
★
  • Corner radius. Minimum 24px, up to full pill capsules at 9999px.
  • Outlines. Every floating functional item carries a 3-4px ink border in #1E293B.
  • Depth. Cards sit on a solid 8px bottom shadow; buttons press down 6px when tapped.
  • Touch targets. 64x64px visual minimum, 72x72px active touch frame.

Core components

The pieces that make up every screen of the app.

Chunky 3D pill buttons

Full pill radius, at least 64px tall, solid fill with a darker shelf and an ink border. They depress 6px on touch with a chime.

Crayon & brush tray

Oversized slanted crayon pegs. The selected crayon pops out 16px with a selection halo and shows its pigment droplet.

Bubbly picture cards

Ultra-rounded 32px cards with bold line-art previews and a golden star medallion when a page is completed.

Adult gate modal

Settings, purchases and external links are shielded by a simple addition problem, styled in subdued pastels to avoid toddler intrigue.

Celebration overlay

Rounded 2D confetti bursts from the artwork while a squishy star stamp bounces in with a rubber-band overshoot.

Palette of crayons

Large, colour-coded swatches with no names on screen - selecting one speaks the colour for the child who can hear it.
