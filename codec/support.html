Frequently Asked Questions

Does Codec upload my data?

No. Every converter, hash, formatter, and generator runs locally on your device using Apple's CryptoKit and on-device engines. Your input, output, and files never leave your phone or Mac.

Which tools are included?

v1 ships with: Base64, URL encode/decode, Hex, MD5/SHA-1/SHA-256/SHA-384/SHA-512/HMAC, File Hash, JSON Formatter, Case Converter, String Inspector, Regex Tester, JWT Decoder, cURL Builder, UUID v4, Password Generator, Timestamp, and Cron "next five" — plus Image ↔ Base64. See the home page for the full list.

Do I need an account?

No. Codec is account-free by design. Open the app, pick a tool, and go.

Does my data sync across devices?

Your favorites sync between your iPhone and Mac via iCloud Key-Value storage, nothing else. Conversion history stays local by default. You can clear all local data from Settings at any time.

Can I use Codec from the Share Sheet?

Yes. The Share Extension and Action Extension let you encode, decode, hash, or timestamp selected text without opening the app. On macOS, you can also pin a tool to the menu bar for instant access.

Is Codec free?

Codec is a paid app with a single one-time purchase that unlocks both iPhone and Mac (universal purchase). No subscriptions, no ads, no upsells.

What are the system requirements?

iOS 17+ on iPhone and iPad, macOS 14+ on Mac. The Share Extension, Action Extension, and menu bar tools are available on both platforms.

Can I request a new tool?

Absolutely — open a GitHub issue using the link above. We triage new tools every release cycle.
